Irrigation and Chemigation Calculators

Free grower tool for estimating irrigation frequency, set time, water depth, water application rate, and common chemigation calculations. The irrigation calculators use effective wetted acres based on the wetted-area percentage entered by the user.

Important: Enter total block acres and the estimated wetted-area percentage. The calculator will estimate effective wetted acres and use that area for set time, water depth, and application-rate calculations. Adjust the percentage based on your field layout, emitter spacing, soil wetting pattern, and grower observations.
